READING THE GRAPH

A walk is a sequence
that respects time.

A two-event walk follows two transactions into the root account. A three-event walk follows three. In both cases, every timestamp must be strictly later than the one before it.

Parallel transactions are separate events. Two events at the same time cannot follow each other in one walk. Protected events stay; mandatory deletions always count toward the cost.
